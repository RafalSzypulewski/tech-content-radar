This roundup looks at how testers can map out their professional growth, starting with a critical but appreciative take on a popular community learning path. It's a reminder that skill maps are useful guides, as long as you read them with a critical eye and don't mistake a list of tools for real competence.

## A Critical Look at the roadmap.sh QA Engineer Path
The author reviews the QA Engineer path on roadmap.sh and points out several flaws: it blurs the line between quality engineering and testing, treats knowing tools and languages as equivalent to having skills, pushes the idea of universal "best practices", works more like a catalogue of skills than an actual roadmap, and its PDF version doesn't match the interactive site. Despite these issues, the review concludes that the roadmap is a living, valuable overview of testing skills, making it a strong starting point for juniors and still worthwhile for mid-level testers who want to structure what they know. The AI-assisted learning features, with topic descriptions, courses and quizzes, are described as a pleasant extra rather than the main draw.
