This roundup centers on one conversation about how AI is changing day-to-day testing work. It covers the practical side, such as handing tasks to agents and building tooling, and the harder questions about responsibility and risk that come with it.

## AB Testing Podcast: Lana on AI Agents, Mobile Automation and Accountability
In this second part of their talk with Lana, Alan Page and Brent Jensen discuss AI-assisted testing, delegating work to agents, and how teams can adjust as their roles shift. Lana describes building a mobile automation tool and checking accessibility on physical devices, and the hosts discuss who stays accountable when AI is involved, the risks it brings, and the ideas behind Alan's forthcoming book. It is useful listening for testers deciding how much to trust agents and how to keep quality ownership clear.
